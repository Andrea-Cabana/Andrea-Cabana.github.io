My Beautiful Keira

A fluffy princess with a heart full of tenderness

Early Days and First Adventures

Meet Keira, my British Longhair Princess! She arrived as a tiny, shy kitten who loved hiding under the bed. But curiosity quickly took over — she explored every room, sniffed every corner, and climbed anywhere she could. Her favorite game was chasing reflections of light, convinced they were magical little creatures.

Personality and Daily Routine

Keira is gentle and sweet, but also playful. She wakes up early, stretches by the window, and waits for breakfast like the elegant queen she is. Afternoon means playtime — feathers, jingling toys, and rolling on the floor. Evenings? She curls up beside me, purring like a tiny engine.

Favorite Toys and Moments

She loves soft toys, anything that jingles, and of course, cardboard boxes! One of her cutest habits is tilting her head when I talk to her, as if she's really trying to understand. Keira is more than a pet — she's my companion, my joy, my fluffy piece of sunshine.

My beautiful Keira

Meet Keira, my British Longhair Princess Keira is an eight-month-old British Longhair cat with the softest white fur and dazzling blue eyes that can melt anyone’s heart. She’s gentle, curious, and full of charm — always ready to explore new corners of the house or take long naps in the sun. Her playful personality and elegant appearance make her the true queen of our home. Through this page, I want to share a little piece of her world — her favorite moments, habits, and the love she brings every day.

Early Days and First Adventures

When Keira first came home, she was a tiny, shy kitten who loved hiding under the bed. It didn’t take long for her curiosity to take over — she started exploring every room, sniffing every corner, and climbing onto places she shouldn’t. Her favorite adventure was chasing the reflection of light on the wall, which she thought was a mysterious moving toy.

Personality and Daily Routine

Keira is calm and sweet, but she also has a playful spirit. She wakes up early, stretches beside the window, and waits patiently for breakfast. Her afternoons are for playtime, usually chasing a feather toy or rolling on the floor with her favorite plush birth. In the evenings, she curls up beside me on the couch — her purring is the best soundtrack to end the day.

Favorite Toys and Moments

Keira loves soft toys, anything that jingles, and cardboard boxes (of course!). She also enjoys watching the world from the window, especially when birds visit the garden. Her most adorable habit is tilting her head when I talk to her, as if she truly understands every word. She’s more than a pet — she’s my little companion, my daily joy, and a fluffy source of happiness.
